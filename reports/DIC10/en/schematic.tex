\par\small Complete X1/INV topology shared by \nolinkurl{01_size_load.sp} and \nolinkurl{02_slew_load.sp}. PMOS S/B connect to vdd; NMOS S/B connect to 0. The gates share in and the drains share out.

The supply is $vdd=VS=1$ V. Dimensions are $W_n=0.6\,\mu\mathrm m\cdot SIZE$, $W_p=1.8\,\mu\mathrm m\cdot SIZE$, and $L=0.25\,\mu$m. The output capacitor is $C_L=\max(LOAD,10^{-21}\mathrm F)$; the tiny numerical floor in the netlist is not a physical load.

VIN is an ideal voltage source to ground: PULSE$(0,1,2\mathrm{ns},SLEW,SLEW,10\mathrm{ns},25\mathrm{ns})$. The first deck sweeps SIZE=1,2,3,4 and LOAD=0,0.1,0.2,0.3 pF at SLEW=0.2 ns. The second fixes SIZE=1 and sweeps the same loads with SLEW=0.2,0.4,0.6,0.8 ns. Connectivity is unchanged across all cases.
