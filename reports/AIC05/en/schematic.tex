\clearpage\section{MOS transistor schematic}
These editable vector schematics connect equal node names within a circuit. Primitive-cell names are local pins; the hierarchy defines their connections. B denotes the body terminal; 0 is global ground. Each panel identifies its netlist and scope; measurement sources are connected as described.
All eight MOS devices of the two-stage amplifier in \texttt{01\_open\_loop\_compensation.sp}.

All $L=0.3\,\mu$m; W in $\mu$m: M1=M2=35, M3=M4=54, M5=2.2, M6=30, M7=269, M8=73. IREF=10 $\mu$A. NMOS bodies are vss; PMOS bodies are vdd. M1 is driven by im and M2 by ip. The source network sets common mode 0.6 V and the differential input/offset specified in the deck. RC=rcomp, CC=ccomp and CL=cload; the baseline uses 1 m$\Omega$, $10^{-21}$ F and 10 pF respectively, then the compensation/load cases alter these parameters. CL returns to global 0, not the AC supply-return node vss. Closed-loop decks reuse this amplifier with different external feedback connections.
